# Country and group metadata: sources and notes

Generated by `uv run python -m pipeline.meta` (code: `pipeline/meta.py`, checks: `tests/test_meta.py`). Notes are current as of 2026-09-28.

## Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `countries.csv` | One row per code in the corpus file names (200) plus every current UN member state (all 193 are in the corpus). |
| `historical_names.csv` | Names used by the entity filed under a code in given years. Outside these ranges the `countries.csv` name applies. |
| `groups.csv` | Office, bloc and region memberships, one row per (group, country). |
| `m49_source.csv` | Input: UN M49 regions (see below). |
| `world-atlas-countries-110m.json` | Input: map geometry (see below). |

CSV files are UTF-8, comma-separated, with `\n` line endings. Read numeric codes as text (leading zeros matter). Booleans are `true`/`false`.

## Sources

- **Corpus codes**: file names in `dataverse_files/TXT/*/*.txt` matching `^[A-Z]{2,4}_\d{2}_\d{4}\.txt$` (UN General Debate Corpus, Jankin, Baturo and Dasandi). A country whose code has changed is filed under its current code (`CODE_RENAMES` in `pipeline/config.py`): Nauru restored its name Naoero at the 81st session (2026) and its code changed from NRU to NRO, so its files `NRU_*` appear as NRO, named Naoero, with "Nauru" in `historical_names.csv` for 2000-2025 (user decision, 2026-09-28). M49 and `pycountry` still list it as NRU (numeric 520), so their data are read under that code.
- **Names**: English short names from `pycountry` (ISO 3166-1, `common_name` where present); Spanish from the `iso-codes` Spanish translation shipped with `pycountry`. Spanish names follow common Latin American usage, not the official UN Spanish forms: "Rusia" rather than "Federación de Rusia", "Turquía" rather than "Türkiye", "Costa de Marfil" rather than "Côte d'Ivoire", "Vietnam", "Nueva Zelanda". Where pycountry's names are official, long or wrong for that reader, `NAME_EN` and `NAME_ES` in `pipeline/meta.py` override them (for example "Rusia", "RD del Congo", "Arabia Saudita", "Rumania", "Qatar", "Myanmar", "Santa Sede", "Palestina"; pycountry misspellings "Burquina Faso", "Costa de Marfíl", "Tunez", "Surinám" and the untranslated "Algeria" are corrected). "Congo" is the Republic of the Congo; "DR Congo" / "RD del Congo" is the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
- **ISO numeric codes**: `m49_source.csv` for current countries; ISO 3166-3 (via `pycountry.historic_countries`) for CSK 200, DDR 278, YMD 720 and YUG 891. ISO 3166-3 gives 891 for the YUCS entry, which covers both Yugoslav federations (the SFRY had used 890). The EU has no code.
- **M49 regions**: `m49_source.csv` from the GitHub repository lukes/ISO-3166-Countries-with-Regional-Codes (`all.csv`), which reproduces the UNSD M49 standard. The `region` and `sub-region` columns are used; "Latin America and the Caribbean" is an M49 sub-region of the Americas. Historical entities take the M49 placement of their territory: CSK and DDR in Eastern Europe (the historical UN classification placed the GDR in Eastern Europe; unified Germany is in Western Europe), YUG in Southern Europe, YMD in Western Asia. The EU has no region.
- **Spanish M49 names**: the UNSD Spanish version ("Asia sudoriental", "América septentrional", "Australia y Nueva Zelandia", etc.), written from reference knowledge and not re-checked online in this pass.
- **UN membership**: the 193 member states (latest admission: South Sudan, 14 July 2011). Observers speaking in the General Debate: the Holy See and the State of Palestine (non-member observer States; Palestine since resolution 67/19 of 29 November 2012, with additional rights from resolution ES-10/23 of 10 May 2024) and the European Union (enhanced observer participation under resolution 65/276 of 3 May 2011, which is why EU speeches start in 2011). `un_member` is current status: false for CSK, DDR, YMD, YUG and the three observers.
- **Map geometry**: `world-atlas-countries-110m.json` (world-atlas TopoJSON, Natural Earth 1:110m), 177 features whose ids are ISO 3166 numeric strings (three have no id). See "Map" below for how codes map to features.

## Map

`countries.csv` has four map columns. Every one of the 177 atlas features is either drawn with a code or listed as a territory below (checked by `tests/test_meta.py`).

- `map_iso_numeric`: id of the code's own atlas feature, when the 110m atlas draws it.
- `map_extra_features`: name (`properties.name`) of one more atlas feature that is shaded and labelled as the same country. These are areas that the United Nations treats as part of a member state but that Natural Earth draws apart:

| Code | Feature | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CYP | N. Cyprus (no id) | Not recognised; Security Council resolution 541 (1983). |
| SOM | Somaliland (no id) | Not recognised; part of Somalia. |
| SRB | Kosovo (no id) | UN documents refer to Kosovo "in the context of Security Council resolution 1244 (1999)". The frontend should keep the feature's outline visible (for example a dashed line) and may add that reference to the hover text. |
| CHN | Taiwan (id 158) | General Assembly resolution 2758 (1971); UN usage "Taiwan Province of China". In 1946-1971 the CHN seat was held by the Republic of China government based in Taiwan, and `historical_names.csv` labels those years "Republic of China"; shading mainland China and Taiwan together in every year follows the rule that codes use today's geometry. |

- `point_lat`, `point_lon`: capital-city coordinates (decimal degrees, WGS 84, two decimals) for the 29 entities with no 110m geometry, so the map can draw them as points: 28 UN member states (AND ATG BHR BRB COM CPV DMA FSM GRD KIR KNA LCA LIE MCO MDV MHL MLT MUS NRU PLW SGP SMR STP SYC TON TUV VCT WSM) and the Holy See. Seven of them are in the ALC group (ATG BRB DMA GRD KNA LCA VCT). The coordinates come from reference knowledge and were not checked against a file in this repository; they are accurate enough for a marker, not for analysis. Empty for every code that has a feature.

Historical entities (CSK, DDR, YMD, YUG) and the EU have no feature and no point, so they are never drawn on the map. CHN and RUS use modern geometry even in years when the code designates the Republic of China or the USSR.

Territories that are not drawn with any code are filled as "no data" and use these labels (UN terminology) instead of the atlas names:

| Atlas feature | Label (en) | Label (es) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Falkland Is. | Falkland Islands (Malvinas) | Islas Malvinas (Falkland) | Non-self-governing territory; sovereignty dispute between Argentina and the United Kingdom (General Assembly resolution 2065 (XX)). Never shade it with ARG or GBR. |
| W. Sahara | Western Sahara | Sáhara Occidental | Non-self-governing territory; not shaded with MAR. |
| New Caledonia | New Caledonia (France) | Nueva Caledonia (Francia) | Non-self-governing territory. |
| Greenland | Greenland (Denmark) | Groenlandia (Dinamarca) | Part of the Kingdom of Denmark, left unshaded so Denmark's values are not spread over its area. |
| Puerto Rico | Puerto Rico (United States) | Puerto Rico (Estados Unidos) | Unshaded, like Greenland. |
| Fr. S. Antarctic Lands | French Southern and Antarctic Lands (France) | Tierras Australes y Antárticas Francesas (Francia) | Unshaded, like Greenland. |
| Antarctica | Antarctica | Antártida | Antarctic Treaty area; may be omitted from the map. |

Added beyond the brief: Siam, Congo (Léopoldville), Central African Empire, Khmer Republic, Tanganyika, Western Samoa, Swaziland, FYROM, and the split of Yugoslavia 1946-1991 at the 1963 constitution.

Changes in the form of a name that a state asked other languages to use, without renaming itself, are not historical names: Ivory Coast to Côte d'Ivoire (1985), Cape Verde to Cabo Verde (2013), Czech Republic to Czechia (2016) and Turkey to Türkiye (2022). In these cases both name columns use one name for all years (the current English form, and the Spanish name).

Flags (simplifications or less certain points):

- YEM 1947-1961: the Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen, which became the Yemen Arab Republic in 1962. "North Yemen" covers both.
- KHM 1975: the speaker (Sihanouk) represented the Royal Government of National Union of Kampuchea, before the Democratic Kampuchea constitution of January 1976, and calls the country "Kampuchea (Cambodia)". The year keeps the modern name "Cambodia", as does 1970.
- BLR and UKR in 1991 were still formally part of the USSR (until December 1991) although their speeches use the new names.
- The Spanish M49 names and the historical M49 placement of the DDR come from reference knowledge, not from a file in this repository.

## Groups

**Fixed-composition rule.** Every group has its current composition (as of 2026-09-26), applied unchanged to all years, 1946 to 2026. A country counts in a group in years before it joined or before the group existed (for example Croatia in UE in 1995, Egypt in BRICS in 1980, RUS in BRICS during the USSR years, DEU in G7 and UE during the West German years). This keeps group series comparable over time; they describe today's groupings looking back, not historical membership.

Historical codes: CSK, DDR and YUG are in no group (they belong to no current bloc, and M49 regions are only grouped for Latin America, Africa and Asia-Pacific). YMD is in ASP (M49 Western Asia). The EU's own delegation (code EU) is in no group, so its speeches are never added to the UE aggregate. The Holy See is in no group.
