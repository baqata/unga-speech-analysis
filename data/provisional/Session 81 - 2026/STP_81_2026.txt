Mr. President, for small island developing states, geographic, economic, financial, and social vulnerabilities are compounded by the effects of climate change. In São Tomé and Príncipe, these impacts are already affecting our coastal areas, infrastructure, marine resources, and livelihoods, despite our negligible contribution to global emissions. This reality reflects a profound climate injustice. São Tomé and Príncipe, therefore, reaffirms its commitments under the Paris Agreement, its nationally determined contribution, and its national adaptation plan, while stressing that their implementation requires adequate, predictable, and accessible resources. We also maintain that the vulnerability of small island states should be assessed beyond per capita income, taking into account their specific characteristics and structural constraints. In this context, we call for the multidimensional vulnerability index to be translated into concrete eligibility criteria for concessional financing. We likewise advocate for the full operationalization of the fund for responding to loss and damage, and simpler, more effective access to the Green Climate Fund.
