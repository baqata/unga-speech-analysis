The `corruption` definition paraphrases ROCOL's description of corruption on the "Corrupción y
delitos económicos" page. The United Nations Convention against Corruption does not itself define
corruption; the lens cites it for the offences, preventive measures, international cooperation and
asset recovery.

The `peace` lens is a project benchmark, not a UNODC or ROCOL mandate area.

## Instruments and resolutions named in anchors and era terms

Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs (1961); Convention on Psychotropic Substances (1971); Convention
against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (1988); General Assembly
special sessions on drugs (1990, 1998 with resolution S-20/4 and the Declaration on the Guiding
Principles of Drug Demand Reduction, 2016); United Nations Guiding Principles on Alternative
Development (2013); Convention for the Suppression of the Traffic in Persons and of the Exploitation
of the Prostitution of Others (1949); United Nations Convention against Transnational Organized
Crime and its Protocols (2000); United Nations Convention against Corruption (2003); General
Assembly special session against corruption (2021); United Nations Convention against Cybercrime
(2024); Tokyo (1963), The Hague (1970) and Montreal (1971) aviation conventions; Declaration on
Measures to Eliminate International Terrorism (1994); Security Council resolution 1373 (2001);
United Nations Global Counter-Terrorism Strategy (2006); Basel Convention (1989); Convention on
International Trade in Endangered Species; Standard Minimum Rules for the Treatment of Prisoners
(1955, revised 2015 as the Nelson Mandela Rules); Bangkok Rules (2010); Kyoto Declaration of the
Fourteenth Crime Congress (2021); An Agenda for Peace (1992).

## Corpus vocabulary check

The era terms were checked against the General Debate corpus (files mentioning each term, by
decade; hyphenated line breaks rejoined; the last five rows were added after review). Examples that shaped the era guidance in `codebook.md`:

| Term | Pattern across decades |
|---|---|
| hijack* | 14 files in the 1960s, 115 in the 1970s, 26 in the 1980s |
| drug abuse | 130 files in the 1980s, 116 in the 1990s, under 10 per decade after 2000 |
| drug traffic* | peaks in the 1990s (336 files) |
| toxic waste | 63 files in the 1980s, 33 in the 1990s |
| organized crime | 12 files in the 1980s, then 150 to 261 per decade from the 1990s |
| money laundering | from the 1990s (78 files) |
| trafficking in persons / human trafficking | from the 2000s |
| violent extremism | 207 files in the 2010s |
| illicit financial flows | only from the 2010s |
| cartel | 10 to 15 files per decade from the 1960s; early uses are commodity cartels |
| drug abuse and illicit traf* | 53 files in the 1980s, 10 in the 1990s; the generic name of the drug problem, hence `drugs` only (codebook 5.2) |
| hostage(s) | 102 files in the 1970s, 147 in the 1980s, 144 in 2020-2025, many of the latest in ceasefire demands (codebook 5.2) |
| State terrorism | 149 files, 71 of them in the 1980s; definition-debate and epithet uses occur in every decade, so the cue is qualified by use, not by era |
| integrity | 4,169 files, 3,342 of them with "territorial integrity"; replaced by "integrity in public office" |
| international crime, gangs, extortion | before about 1990 mostly aggression, apartheid or invective against adversaries ("racist gangs", "Zionist gang") |
